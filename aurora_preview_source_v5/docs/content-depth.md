# Content check, November 7, 2026

This file describes the built 111-URL architecture. It is not a keyword-volume study, and a repeated sentence is not a local fact.

## Correction

An earlier draft said Byers and Watkins were both unincorporated Adams County and sent `/areas/byers/` to `/areas/watkins/`. That was wrong.

The Census Bureau place list records:

- Byers CDP in Arapahoe County only.
- Watkins CDP in Adams County and Arapahoe County.
- Strasburg CDP in Adams County and Arapahoe County.
- Bennett town, an incorporated place, in Adams County and Arapahoe County.

Arapahoe County's Byers Subarea Plan treats Byers as unincorporated Arapahoe County and matches its planning area to the census place. A Byers, Watkins, Bennett, or Strasburg mailing address is still not the parcel. The permit office is the town, if the address is inside a town, or the county that contains the unincorporated parcel. Aurora applies only when the parcel is inside Aurora.

The same draft sent Franktown, Sedalia, Stonegate, Meridian, and Roxborough Park to a page titled The Pinery because Douglas County is the building counter. A shared permit office is not the same homeowner question. Those URLs are separate pages again. Each names the district that publishes service for its own customers, and each says the census name and the ZIP code are not that district boundary.

## What is not a differentiator

The same closing links, the same “confirm the bill” sentence, and the same county phone do not make 48 unique city investigations. A useful local fact on these pages is one of the following, and only where the source says it:

- Census county for the place, kept separate from the ZIP code.
- The building counter that applies after the parcel is checked.
- A district’s own description of what it serves, including the cases where service is not uniform.

Where a permit page does not name the pipe owner, the page says to confirm the utility. That uncertainty is the fact. It is not counted as a unique local rule.
