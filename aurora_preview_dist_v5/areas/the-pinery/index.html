Douglas County

Plumber The Pinery CO

The Pinery is its own census place and its own water district. It is not a label for Franktown, Sedalia, Stonegate, Meridian, or Roxborough Park.

Pinery Water's own list

Pinery Water and Wastewater District, which began as Denver Southeast Suburban Water and Sanitation District, says it serves the Pinery, Timbers, Misty Pines, Pinery Glen, High Prairie Farms, Pradera, Vistancia, and Colorado Golf Club. The office is 5242 Old Schoolhouse Road, Parker, phone 303-841-2797. That list is the district's. It is not the census boundary, and a Parker mailing address is not the Town of Parker. The district's water-quality page reports average hardness of 210 milligrams per liter and says the district does not treat for hardness. That figure describes the district's supply. It is not a test of your tap and not a reason, by itself, to buy a softener.

Building permits

If the parcel is unincorporated Douglas County, the building permit goes to Douglas County at 100 Third Street, Castle Rock, 303-660-7497. That is not Castle Rock's tankless packet and not the Town of Parker's counter. Franktown is not on Pinery Water's community list. Use the Franktown, Sedalia, Stonegate, Meridian, and Roxborough Park pages for those places. Confirm the bill before you authorize work on a line in the street.

Questions to settle first

Is my house a Pinery Water customer because the area is called The Pinery?

Only if the district serves that parcel. Pinery Water names the Pinery and seven other communities. A nearby census place is not on that list. The office is 303-841-2797 in Parker. The Parker mailing city is not the Town of Parker's building department.

Who permits a water heater?

Douglas County, if the parcel is unincorporated, at 303-660-7497. The office is in Castle Rock and is still the county. Inside the Town of Castle Rock, the town's tankless rules apply instead. Confirm the parcel before you prepare a gas-load sheet.

Does the district's hardness number mean I need a softener?

No. The district reports 210 milligrams per liter as the average hardness of its supply and says it does not treat for hardness. That is not a test at your faucet. A filter or a softener is a separate decision after a test of the water you actually use.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
