City of Louisville

Plumber Louisville CO

Louisville can turn a water heater or a sewer repair around the same day or the next business day. An addition is measured in weeks. The contractor needs a Louisville license.

Clocks

The building page lists same-day or next-business-day review for a water heater, a minor plumbing repair, and a sewer or water repair. A gas line or a hot tub is on a one-to-two-week fast track. A residential addition or a new home is four to six weeks. Over-the-counter permits are paid when you apply. Plan-review permits are paid after approval, and the city says the permit then takes about one business day to issue. Inspection requests are for the next business day. Contractors must hold a current Louisville license and be named on the application.

Taps and mines

New water and sewer connections use fixture-count forms and a tap-fee calculation. That is not a repair of an existing lateral. The city also tells you to check a mine-hazard map before you build. A plumbing quote that ignores a mine note on an addition is incomplete. Ownership of an existing sewer lateral was not on the permit page. Ask the utility. Do not use Aurora's rule.

Questions to settle first

How fast is a Louisville water heater permit?

The city lists water heaters with same-day or next-business-day permits, in the same group as a sewer or water repair. An addition is four to six weeks. Pay over-the-counter permits when you apply. Those clocks are targets, not a promise from this guide.

Does my plumber need a city license?

Yes. The page says contractors and subcontractors must hold a current Louisville license and be listed on the application. A license from another city is not what that sentence says. Ask to see the Louisville license before the work starts.

Is a tap fee the same as a sewer repair?

No. Fixture-count and tap-fee forms are for connections. A repair of an existing line is the same-day group if it is only a repair. The page does not say who owns the pipe in the street. Ask before you include a patch.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
