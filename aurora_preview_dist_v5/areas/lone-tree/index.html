City of Lone Tree

Plumber Lone Tree CO

Lone Tree does not sell water or sewer. The city's utility page is a starting map, not proof of the district at a single address. The bill or the district's own lookup controls.

Which repair → Quote checklist →

The bill names the district

The city's utility providers page says the city does not provide water or sewer. It lists Southgate Water and Sanitation Districts for property west of I-25, Parker Water and Sanitation District for property east of I-25, and Northern Douglas County Water and Sanitation District for some homes in Terra Ridge along Indian Wells Drive. A resident guide has also said Parker Water serves all Lone Tree properties east of I-25. That "all" is broader than the utility page, which already names a third district. Use the address and the district, not the highway alone. Southgate's emergency number in the resident guide is 303-799-0261. Parker Water's number there is 303-841-4627. Those are district numbers, not this site. Parker Water's service-line boundary is unresolved on the Parker page. Do not inherit a sidewalk rule. Building permits, including a water heater, go through Lone Tree at 720-390-5211. Inspections are scheduled at 303-225-4941.

Do not use Parker's town counter

Being a Parker Water customer does not make the house inside the Town of Parker. The Town of Parker permits only inside its limits. A Lone Tree house can have a Lone Tree building permit and any of those three districts. Ask both the city and the district named on the bill. Aurora's tap rule is a third error.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

Who is my sewer district in Lone Tree?

Do not decide from the highway alone. The city's utility page lists Southgate west of I-25, Parker Water east of I-25, and Northern Douglas County Water for some Terra Ridge homes on Indian Wells Drive. A resident guide's broader 'all east of I-25' line is why you confirm with the district or the bill. The phones in the guide are the districts', not this site.

Does Parker Water mean the Town of Parker inspects the heater?

No. Lone Tree's building division permits water heaters inside Lone Tree, at 720-390-5211. The Town of Parker permits inside Parker. The water district and the building department are different. A tankless review in Castle Rock is a third city.

Where can the dumpster sit?

On your property, a temporary permit is required unless a building permit is already active. In the street, Public Works wants a right-of-way permit. The guide also says the dumpster cannot sit in the right of way for more than 30 consecutive days in a year unless construction is active.
