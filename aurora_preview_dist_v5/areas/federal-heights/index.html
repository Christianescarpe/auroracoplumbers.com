City of Federal Heights

Plumber Federal Heights CO

Federal Heights inspects its own building permits, and it sends electrical permits to the state. A water heater that also needs a new circuit is two authorities, not one.

City building, state electrical

The building department says the State of Colorado, through DORA, administers all electrical permits and inspections in Federal Heights. Schedule a city building inspection by 3:30 p.m. the day before, at 303-412-3534, with the permit number and the type of inspection. The office is at 2380 West 90th Avenue. The city also publishes a mobile-home set application, so a manufactured home on a lot here is a city building question plus whatever the home's under-floor piping needs. See mobile home plumbing for the limits of that page.

What was not in the building blurb

The page does not name the water or sewer utility and does not say who owns a lateral. Ask the utility on the bill before a street cut. Do not borrow Aurora, Thornton, or Westminster. A plumbing repair that is only a faucet may still be a city building permit. An electrical reconnection is the state's permit, not a line you add quietly.

Questions to settle first

Who permits the wiring for a water heater in Federal Heights?

The state. The city says DORA administers all electrical permits and inspections here. The city's own inspectors still cover the building permit. Call 303-412-3534 by 3:30 p.m. the day before for a city inspection. Do not expect the city counter to issue the electrical permit.

Is there a mobile-home permit?

The building page lists a mobile home set application. That is about setting the home, not a promise about under-floor pipe fittings. The lot is still in Federal Heights if the parcel is. Ask the building department which permit covers a plumbing change after the home is set.

Who owns the sewer line?

Federal Heights' building page does not say. Do not invent a curb line. Get the utility name from the bill, then ask that utility who repairs the pipe under the street. Responsibility varies by utility. Confirm it before you authorize the work.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
