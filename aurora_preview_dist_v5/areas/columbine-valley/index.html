Town of Columbine Valley

Plumber Columbine Valley CO

Columbine Valley's building department is the counter Bow Mar uses. The address is 2 Middlefield Road in Columbine Valley. The water and sewer utility is whoever bills the house.

The shared counter

Bow Mar's permit page says its building review is handled by Columbine Valley at 2 Middlefield Road, phone 303-795-2063. That places a building department in Columbine Valley that processes at least Bow Mar's permits and, by the same address, is the town's own counter. A water heater, a furnace, and gas-line changes are on Bow Mar's non-structural list filed through that office. Confirm with 303-795-2063 whether a Columbine Valley address uses the same non-structural list or a stricter one. Do not assume the exemption for minor drain repairs applies inside Columbine Valley until the town says so.

Utility

No water or sanitation district was named on the Bow Mar page. Columbine Valley is not Littleton and not Denver, even when the mail is confusing. Ask the bill. A street excavation may be a different agency from the building permit. Bow Mar is the neighbor that shares this counter. Littleton has its own sewer code if the parcel is actually in Littleton.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

Is the Columbine Valley building counter the same as Bow Mar's?

Bow Mar says its permits are handled by Columbine Valley at 2 Middlefield Road, 303-795-2063. Use that office and ask whether your Columbine Valley address is on the same non-structural list as a Bow Mar water heater. Do not file in Aurora or Littleton until the parcel is checked.

Does the minor-repair exemption apply?

Bow Mar's page says drain and pipe repairs need a permit when the trap, drain, or pipe is replaced. That sentence is Bow Mar's. Ask Columbine Valley before you rely on it for a house inside Columbine Valley. Replacing a trap is the line they drew for Bow Mar.

Who provides water?

Not stated on the page that identified the building counter. Do not guess Denver Water or a sanitation district. The bill is the source. Ownership of a lateral is a separate question this page will not invent. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.
