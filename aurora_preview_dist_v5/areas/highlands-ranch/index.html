Unincorporated Douglas County · not a city building department

Plumber Highlands Ranch CO

Highlands Ranch is not a city with its own building counter. Permits for the unincorporated community go through Douglas County. The water and sewer provider is a separate question, and the County tells owners to verify it before they dig.

Which repair → Quote checklist →

The permit desk is the County's

Douglas County's building permits page says permits are issued for unincorporated areas of the county. Contractors who apply have to be in the County's registration program. The owner or permit holder is responsible for verifying utilities and easements, including water, sewer, well, septic, electric, and gas, before excavation. The Building Division phone on that page is 303-660-7497, at 100 Third Street in Castle Rock. Applications can go to eplans@douglasco.gov. That email is the County's, not an inbox for this website, and there is no link here that sends a message.

Do not drive the job to Aurora's permit center, and do not assume the Town of Castle Rock's eTRAKiT account covers a Highlands Ranch house. Castle Rock's tankless worksheet is for the Town. It is linked from Castle Rock because the towns are easy to confuse when the County's office sits in Castle Rock's downtown.

What this changes on a plumbing quote

A water heater or a sewer repair needs the County permit path, not Aurora's same-day sentence and not Centennial's one-stop list. Ask the contractor to name Douglas County as the building authority when the parcel is unincorporated. Ask which water or sanitation district, or which well, serves the lot. This page does not name a single Highlands Ranch utility, because the County's instruction is to verify the address. A well is possible in Douglas County and is not the default. Do not order well-pump work because the community sounds suburban.

Dumpsters, the County notes, stay on the property or the driveway, not on a public road. That is a small access fact that belongs in a replacement or a sewer dig, not a landmark tour.

What the County lists, and what it does not decide

The permit page lists mechanical and plumbing work as its own application type, next to electrical, roofs, and new homes. It does not publish, on that page, a one-stop water-heater exception like Centennial's schedule, and it does not publish Aurora's same-day heater sentence. Treat a Highlands Ranch heater as a County mechanical or plumbing permit until the counter says otherwise. The page also says applications received after 4 p.m. are post-dated to the next business day. That is a scheduling fact, not a service promise. Fees are on a separate County table that this guide does not copy, because fee tables go stale.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

Is there a Highlands Ranch building department?

Not as a city counter for this community. Douglas County issues building permits in unincorporated areas, and Highlands Ranch is unincorporated. The phone on the County permit page is 303-660-7497. An Aurora or Castle Rock permit assumption is the wrong office until someone shows the parcel is inside one of those cities.

Who is the water utility?

This page will not name one for every Highlands Ranch address. The County says the owner or permit holder must verify water, sewer, well, and septic locations before digging. Ask for the district or the well record on the quote. Aurora Water's tap rule does not come with the mailing address.

Why is the County office in Castle Rock?

The Building Division's published address is 100 Third Street, Castle Rock. That is the County's office location. It does not mean the Town of Castle Rock is permitting a Highlands Ranch house. Town tankless reviews and County mechanical permits are different stacks. Match the permit to the parcel.
