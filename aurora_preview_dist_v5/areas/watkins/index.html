Adams County and Arapahoe County

Plumber Watkins CO

Watkins is a census-designated place in both Adams County and Arapahoe County. It is not a city, and a Watkins mailing address is not a permit jurisdiction.

The name on the mail is not the parcel

The Census Bureau's place list records Watkins CDP in Adams County and Arapahoe County. That outline is a statistical area. It is not ZIP code 80137, and it is not every house that receives Watkins mail. An address can sit in unincorporated Adams County, unincorporated Arapahoe County, or inside a city. Aurora's building division applies only when the parcel is inside Aurora. There is no Watkins city hall.

Which counter, after the parcel is checked

If the parcel is unincorporated Adams County, Adams County requires a permit to install or replace a water heater, to modify plumbing, or to run a gas line. Building Safety is 720-523-6800, through the county ePermit center. If the parcel is unincorporated Arapahoe County, Arapahoe County Building applies. A water heater that is not tankless can be issued when you pay. A tankless heater is a reviewed remodel, with a gas-pipe isometric and the manufacturer's instructions. The phone on those residential instructions is 303-795-4400, at 5334 South Prince Street in Littleton. That is the county office. It does not mean the house is in Littleton. The same filing split is on the Cherry Creek CDP page because that place is also unincorporated Arapahoe land.

The utility is separate

Neither county's permit list names a Watkins water or sewer district. The bill, a well, or a septic system tells you who is responsible for the pipe. Responsibility varies by utility. Confirm the district before you authorize work in the street. A building permit is permission to do the plumbing. It is not a map of who owns the main. Byers is a different census place, in Arapahoe County, and it is not this page.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

Who permits a water heater at a Watkins address?

Whoever has the parcel. Census lists the Watkins census place in Adams County and Arapahoe County, and a Watkins ZIP can also fall inside a city. Unincorporated Adams uses Building Safety at 720-523-6800. Unincorporated Arapahoe uses the county building division at 303-795-4400. Aurora permits the job only when the parcel is inside Aurora.

Is Watkins a city?

No. It is an unincorporated census place, not a municipal building department. The name on the mail does not choose Adams County, Arapahoe County, or Aurora. Look up the parcel, then file with that government. A contractor who picks the nearest counter because the drive is short can file in the wrong county.

Who owns the sewer?

The permit instructions do not say. A district, a well, or a septic system may serve the lot, and neighboring houses need not match. Confirm the utility on the bill before you authorize a street repair. A house permit covers the work you are allowed to do. It does not decide who maintains the main.
