Adams County and Arapahoe County

Plumber Strasburg CO

Strasburg is a census-designated place in both Adams County and Arapahoe County. It is not a city. The ZIP code does not pick the county or the permit office.

Two counties, one postal name

The Census Bureau's place list records Strasburg CDP in Adams County and Arapahoe County. The census place is not the ZIP code, and a Strasburg mailing address can fall outside that outline. If the parcel is unincorporated Adams County, Adams County requires permits for water heater replacement, plumbing changes, and gas lines. Building Safety is 720-523-6800. If the parcel is unincorporated Arapahoe County, use Arapahoe County Building at 303-795-4400. A non-tankless water heater there can be issued when you pay. A tankless heater is a reviewed remodel. Aurora permits the job only when the parcel is inside Aurora.

Water source

Those permit lists do not name a Strasburg water or sewer district. Ask whether the house is on a district main, a well, or a septic system before you order parts. Heater replacement follows the county that has the parcel. Responsibility for the pipe varies by utility. Confirm the bill before you authorize street work. Distance from Aurora is not an Aurora Water account.

Questions to settle first

Which building department is Strasburg?

The government that contains the parcel. Census lists the Strasburg census place in Adams County and Arapahoe County, and the place is not a city. Unincorporated Adams uses 720-523-6800. Unincorporated Arapahoe uses 303-795-4400. A Strasburg ZIP is not that choice. Confirm the parcel before you pay a fee.

Does Aurora Water apply?

Only if the bill says so. Proximity is not an account. A sewer backup number for Aurora is the wrong desk for a Strasburg district or a septic system. Identify the provider first. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

Are gas lines permitted?

On unincorporated land, plan on a permit. Adams County lists gas lines with work that needs one. In unincorporated Arapahoe, a new gas line is outside the non-review appliance permit. Neither sentence is a how-to. If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
