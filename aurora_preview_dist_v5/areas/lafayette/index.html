City of Lafayette

Plumber Lafayette CO

Lafayette requires a permit to replace plumbing systems, and it also lists repairs to an existing water heater, and same-size sink or toilet swaps, among work that can be exempt. Read the exception before you skip the permit.

Exempt versus quick

The city's permit page says a permit is required to install or replace electrical, gas, mechanical, or plumbing systems. It then lists exemptions, including maintenance or repairs to a water heater, same-size replacement of a sink or toilet, and repairing leaks or stoppages in water or sewer piping. A quick-permit list, estimated at two business days, includes water heaters, water and sewer repairs, furnaces, and plumbing or mechanical work without engineered plans. Replacing a water heater may be the quick permit, while a small repair of the same heater may be the exemption. Ask Building at 303-661-1451 which box your scope is. Do not guess. The city hall number is 303-665-5588 at 1290 South Public Road.

Not Erie, not Boulder

Those neighbors have different clocks. Lafayette's two-business-day estimate is not Boulder’s mechanical-permit category and not Aurora's same-day request. Ownership of the lateral was not on this permit page. Ask the city's utility. A stoppage you clear without replacing pipe is the exemption they wrote. Replacing the pipe is new work.

Questions to settle first

Do I need a permit to repair a Lafayette water heater?

The page lists maintenance or repairs to a water heater as exempt, and it also lists water heaters on a quick-permit track of about two business days. The difference is repair versus replacement. Call Building at 303-661-1451 before you skip the permit. Replacing pipe is not the stoppage exemption.

Is a clog exempt?

Repairing leaks or stoppages in water or sewer piping is on the exempt list. If you have to remove and replace a concealed drain, that is new work and needs a permit. The city's sentence is similar in spirit to Boulder's, and it is still Lafayette's list. Use Lafayette's phone.

How fast is a sewer repair permit?

Water and sewer repairs are on the quick-permit list, with an estimated two business days. That is a short review, not permission to bury the work. The timing page does not say who owns the lateral. Ask the utility before you patch the street.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
