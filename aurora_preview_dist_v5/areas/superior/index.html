Town of Superior

Plumber Superior CO

Superior's building division is operated by SAFEbuilt. Paper applications are not accepted. The permit goes through CommunityCore.

How you file

The town says a building permit is pulled in CommunityCore and that paper submittals are not accepted. SAFEbuilt runs the building division: plan review, permits, and inspections. Permitting email is the SAFEbuilt address the town prints, superiorinspections@safebuilt.com. Building phone is 303-381-2028. Planning is 303-499-3675 extension 131, at 100 Superior Plaza Way, Suite 200. Contractor licensing is a separate town page. The office will not take a stack of paper plans at the desk.

The bill names the utility

The planning instructions do not name the water or sewer district, and they do not set a property-line rule for the pipe. Ask the utility on the bill before a sewer or water line quote includes the street. A water heater still needs the permit SAFEbuilt requires. Louisville's same-day list is Louisville's. Paper plans left at the front desk are not an application.

Questions to settle first

Can I drop off paper plans in Superior?

No. The town says applications and submittals go through CommunityCore and paper is not accepted. SAFEbuilt reviews and inspects. The building phone is 303-381-2028. A contractor who is not set up in the portal will delay a water heater that could have been filed online.

Who provides sewer?

Superior's planning instructions do not name the district. Use the bill rather than a neighbor city's utility. The building permit and the district's pipe are different permissions. Confirm both before the street is opened. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

Is Superior's heater permit the same as Louisville's?

No. Louisville publishes a same-day list. Superior publishes an electronic portal run by SAFEbuilt. Ask Superior what a replacement heater requires. Aurora's inspection sentence does not apply. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
