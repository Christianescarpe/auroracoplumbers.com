Douglas County

Plumber Meridian CO

Meridian Metropolitan District provides drinking water, wastewater, and reclaimed irrigation water to its customers. A Meridian address is not automatically that district, and it is not the City of Lone Tree.

What the district says it provides

Meridian Metropolitan District's water page says it provides drinking water, wastewater, and reclaimed water for irrigation. The phone on that page is 303-790-0345. The office address printed there is 12111 East Belford Avenue, Englewood. That is the district office. It does not mean the house is in Englewood, and it does not mean Lone Tree's building division takes the permit. The district boundary is the account, not the word Meridian on a map.

Building permits

Census lists Meridian CDP in Douglas County. If the parcel is unincorporated, Douglas County Building is at 100 Third Street, Castle Rock, 303-660-7497. Lone Tree permits land inside Lone Tree. Englewood permits land inside Englewood. Pinery Water is a different district. Confirm the bill before you authorize work on a line in the street. Responsibility varies by utility. This page does not copy the district's rate table.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

Is every Meridian address a Meridian Metropolitan District customer?

No. The district describes the services it provides to its customers. The census place and a marketing name can be wider than the legal boundary. Check the bill or call 303-790-0345. An Englewood office address is the district's office, not your city.

Who permits a water heater?

Douglas County, if the parcel is unincorporated, at 303-660-7497. Lone Tree's building division applies inside Lone Tree. Englewood applies inside Englewood. The water district does not replace that permit. Confirm the parcel before you file. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

Does the district handle irrigation as well as sewer?

For its customers, the district says it provides drinking water, wastewater, and reclaimed water for irrigation. Reclaimed irrigation is not the drinking-water line and not a sump pump. Ask which system the quote is opening before work starts. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.
