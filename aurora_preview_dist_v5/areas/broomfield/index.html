City and County of Broomfield

Plumber Broomfield CO

Broomfield is its own city and county. Every water heater, new or replacement, needs a permit. A new water or sewer tap license is not issued before the building permit.

Heaters and sewer repairs

The building division says all water heaters need a permit, including replacements, and it points homeowners to a state addendum on residential plumbing work. Sewer line repairs are on the same permit list as gas lines, boilers, and heat pumps. Email applications to buildingpermits@broomfield.org. This is not Aurora, and it is not a quick verbal okay.

New taps versus a repair

City council ordinances described on the page say a water license and a sewer license may be applied for with the building permit and shall not be issued before that building permit. The page also states 2026 license amounts for a new three-quarter-inch water tap and an equivalent sewer tap. Those are connection licenses, not a plumber's repair price, and this guide does not repeat the dollar figures as a quote. A leaking existing service line is not a new tap. Ask which application you are buying.

Questions to settle first

Does a replacement water heater need a permit in Broomfield?

Yes. The building division says every water heater needs one, new or replacement. Homeowners are pointed to the state's addendum on residential plumbing work. File with the building division. Do not use Aurora's same-day language or Denver's quick-permit list.

Can I buy a water tap before the building permit?

No. The city says water and sewer licenses are applied for with the building permit and are not issued before it. That rule is about a new tap, not about patching a leak on a line you already have. Ask the counter which file you are in.

Is a sewer repair a tap license?

No. Sewer line repairs are listed as building-permit work. A new sewer license is a different, larger connection fee discussed on the same page. Do not let a repair quote cite a new-tap license unless you are actually adding service.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
