Douglas County

Plumber Franktown CO

Franktown is a census-designated place in Douglas County. It is not a town, and it is not on Pinery Water's published list of communities.

Permits follow the parcel

If the parcel is unincorporated Douglas County, the Building Division is at 100 Third Street in Castle Rock, phone 303-660-7497. That office sits in Castle Rock and is not the Town of Castle Rock's permit desk. The county expects the owner to verify water, sewer, well, and septic before digging. Applications after 4 p.m. are dated the next business day. A house inside Castle Rock, Parker, or another city uses that city instead. A Franktown mailing address is not that proof.

Water

Franktown has no town water utility. Pinery Water publishes the communities it serves, and Franktown is not on that list. Douglas County's water-provider map shows many other districts across the county. A Franktown address may be a well, a small district, or another provider. The bill or the well record decides. Do not assign Parker Water or Aurora Water because those systems are down the highway. Sedalia, Stonegate, Meridian, and Roxborough Park each have their own district to check. Sharing a county permit office does not make them the same water system.

Questions to settle first

Who permits a Franktown water heater?

Douglas County, if the parcel is unincorporated, at 303-660-7497. The office is in Castle Rock and is still the county, not the town. If the parcel is inside a city, use that city. A Franktown mailing address is not the parcel check.

Is Franktown on Pinery Water?

Not on the community list Pinery Water publishes. That list names the Pinery and several neighboring developments, not Franktown. Read the bill. A well is possible. A district is possible. Neither is guaranteed by the census name. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

Does Castle Rock's tankless packet apply?

Only inside the Town of Castle Rock. An unincorporated Franktown parcel uses the county. The town's manufacturer sheet and gas-load calculations are the town's requirement, not a county-wide form. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
