Town of Foxfield

Plumber Foxfield CO

Foxfield is its own town, a few miles from Aurora, and it is not Aurora's permit counter. Water heaters and water or sewer lines are on the town's one-stop list.

One-stop list

The town's permit page says no work starts until a permit is approved. One-stop items include a water heater, a water or sewer line, a lawn sprinkler, a furnace, and an air conditioner. The town says it enforces the 2015 international building codes. That date is what the page currently states. Confirm it before you design to a newer book, because the page may lag. Inspections are done by SAFEbuilt, not by Aurora's inspectors. Zoning is Chapter 16 of the town code.

Utility not named

The permit page does not name a water or sanitation district. A Foxfield address can be easy to confuse with an Aurora water account. Check the bill. A water line or sewer line on the one-stop list still needs the district's answer on who owns the pipe in the street. This page will not invent that line.

Questions to settle first

Is a Foxfield water heater an Aurora permit?

No. Foxfield lists water heaters on its own one-stop list, and SAFEbuilt inspects for the town. Aurora's building division does not cover a parcel inside Foxfield. Confirm the town limit. The codes page says the town enforces the 2015 international codes. Verify that edition is still what they want.

Who provides sewer?

Not stated on the permit page. Do not assume Aurora Water because the town is nearby. Use the bill, then ask that district who maintains the lateral. The one-stop sewer-line permit is the town's building permit, not the district's ownership map.

Can I start and permit later?

The town says no project starts until the permit is approved, and that working without one can mean a fine and a stop-work order. Brighton's next-day emergency exception is Brighton's, not Foxfield's. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
