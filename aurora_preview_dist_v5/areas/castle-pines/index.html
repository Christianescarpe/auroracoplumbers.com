City of Castle Pines

Plumber Castle Pines CO

Castle Pines has its own building department. That page does not name the water district. Use the bill, and do not guess a neighboring city's utility.

The counter

The city building department issues permits and inspections and can be reached at 303-705-0227, weekdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., or buildingdept@castlepinesco.gov. The office is at 7437 Village Square Drive, Suite 200. That is not Douglas County's counter at 100 Third Street in Castle Rock, and it is not the Town of Castle Rock's residential permit desk. A house inside the city uses the city. A house in an unincorporated Douglas County pocket nearby, such as The Pinery if that is the real jurisdiction, uses the county. Confirm the parcel.

Utility unknown on that page

Because the building page did not name a water or sanitation provider, do not assume Castle Rock Water, Parker Water, or Aurora Water. Ask the city or the bill. A water heater permit is a building question. A sewer repair in the street is a utility question this page refuses to guess. Castle Rock's tankless gas-load sheet applies in Castle Rock, not here, unless Castle Pines tells you they adopted the same packet.

Questions to settle first

Is Castle Pines the same permit office as Castle Rock?

No. Castle Pines publishes its own building department at 303-705-0227 on Village Square Drive. Castle Rock's tankless rules are the Town of Castle Rock's. Douglas County permits unincorporated land from a different counter. Match the parcel before you upload plans.

Who provides sewer?

The Castle Pines building page does not say. Do not assign Castle Rock Water, Parker Water, or Aurora Water because they are nearby. Use the bill. A street-cut quote without the utility's name is incomplete. Confirm the district before you authorize the work.

Does the city inspect water heaters?

The building department says it issues permits and does inspections. Ask that office whether a replacement heater is a simple permit or a review. This page will not copy another city's one-stop list onto Castle Pines. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
