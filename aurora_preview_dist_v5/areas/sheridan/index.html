City of Sheridan

Plumber Sheridan CO

Sheridan requires a building permit for every water heater replacement, and it publishes constraints for closets, combustion air, and tankless vents that Aurora's pages do not.

Which repair → Quote checklist →

The permit stays visible

The city's water-heater page says every replacement needs a building permit, the permittee leaves it on site visible from the street, and the permittee requests the inspection. Installations must follow the 2021 residential or plumbing code, and the heater must be listed for the place it sits. Fuel-fired replacements are not allowed in storage closets. A heater in a sleeping room or bathroom must be in a sealed enclosure with its own combustion air. A gas heater in a room under 250 square feet needs combustion air. A tankless unit is allowed as a replacement only if the gas piping is large enough and the vent follows the manufacturer. This guide will not repeat those constraints as installation steps. They are the city's conditions. Over-the-counter permits on the applications page include sewer line repairs. Plumbing permits are required for new installations or replacements.

Ownership not copied

Those pages do not say who owns the lateral under the street. Do not use Aurora or Englewood. Ask Sheridan's utility. The building rules above are already enough to change a heater quote and a tankless quote.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

Does Sheridan allow a gas water heater in a bedroom closet?

Not as a storage closet, and not in a sleeping room or bathroom unless it is in a sealed enclosure with its own combustion air. A room under 250 square feet needs combustion air for a gas heater. The city says to call its building department if that is a hardship. This page will not tell you how to build the enclosure.

Is a tankless heater allowed as a replacement?

Yes, if the gas piping is adequate and the vent is installed as the manufacturer requires. That is a sizing question for the contractor, not a do-it-yourself worksheet. The permit is still required and must be visible from the street.

Is a sewer repair over the counter?

The applications page lists sewer line repairs with over-the-counter permits, paid in full. A new plumbing installation still needs the plumbing permit. Who owns the pipe in the street was not on those pages. Ask the utility before you patch asphalt.
