City of Wheat Ridge

Plumber Wheat Ridge CO

Wheat Ridge requires a building permit to replace a furnace, a boiler, or a water heater. Since January 2025 the application lives in OpenGov, and a permit is valid for 180 days.

What needs a permit

The building page lists water heater replacement with furnaces and boilers, and it also lists in-ground and above-ground pools and hot tubs deeper than 24 inches, interior remodels, and basement finishes. The permit desk is 303-235-2855, option 1. Permits last 180 days, with another 180 days if an approved inspection shows real progress, under the code section the city cites. A like-for-like faucet is not on that list. A water heater is. Do not treat Wheat Ridge as Aurora's same-day program.

Districts are extra

The building page does not name one water or sewer owner. Wheat Ridge sits among several special districts. Ask who bills the water before you dig. A pool deeper than 24 inches is a building permit here even when the work is the shell, not just a leaking union. Who owns the pipe is the district's fact.

Questions to settle first

Does Wheat Ridge require a permit for a replacement water heater?

Yes. The building page lists furnace, boiler, and water heater replacement with work that needs a permit, filed in OpenGov since January 2025. The permit lasts 180 days unless an inspection shows progress and extends it. The desk is 303-235-2855, option 1. That is the city's number.

Is a hot tub included?

A hot tub or pool deeper than 24 inches, in the ground or above it, is on the permit list. A shallow portable unit may not be. Ask before you set it. The plumbing of the pipes is still separate from the electrical connection.

Who maintains the sewer lateral?

Not answered on the building page. Do not copy Aurora or Westminster. Identify the district on the bill. A permit to do the work is not the same fact as who pays for the pipe under the street. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
