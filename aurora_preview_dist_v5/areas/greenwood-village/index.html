City of Greenwood Village

Plumber Greenwood Village CO

Greenwood Village will not name your water or sewer provider at the front counter without a map. The building permit also wants proof you submitted to that district.

Find the district, then the permit

Public Works says to use the water district map and the sanitation district map, or call 303-708-6100. The sanitation map the city publishes includes Castlewood, Cherry Creek Valley, Southgate, Goldsmith Gulch, and others, and it flags some parcels that may have a septic system and should call Arapahoe County Public Health. Work in the right of way needs a permit from 10001 East Costilla Avenue. Community Development, 303-486-5783 at 6060 South Quebec Street, says a building permit is generally required for an addition, alteration, or repair, and that kitchen cabinets alone may still need a plumbing permit if fixtures are reinstalled.

External sign-off

The permit instructions say proof of submittal to the water and sanitation district is included when it applies, and that the district's approval does not replace the village review. A quote that skips the district letter can stall the permit. Do not assume Aurora Water or Denver Water. Ask the map.

Questions to settle first

How do I find the sewer district in Greenwood Village?

Use the city's sanitation district map or call Public Works at 303-708-6100. The map includes more than one district, and some parcels may be on septic and are told to call Arapahoe County Public Health. Aurora's utility is not the default.

Does a new faucet need a permit?

The building page says cabinet work may still need a plumbing permit when fixtures are reinstalled. Painting and floor coverings do not. Call Community Development at 303-486-5783 before you open the shutoffs. A full bathroom is an alteration and is a permit.

Can the district approve the job instead of the village?

No. The permit page says outside approvals do not replace village plan review. You may need both the district and the city. A right-of-way cut is a third permit, from Public Works on Costilla. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
