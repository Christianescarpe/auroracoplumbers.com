City of Northglenn

Plumber Northglenn CO

Northglenn puts water heaters and sewers on a one-stop list, and it publishes a multi-week clock for a residential plan review. Those are city targets, not a promise this guide can make.

One-stop versus plan review

The building page lists one-stop permits that include water heaters, sewers, furnaces, and air conditioners, filed in CommunityCore. Residential plan review is listed at 10 to 14 business days. A commercial job under one million dollars is 14 to 20 business days, and a larger commercial job is 21 to 30. Building Division hours are weekdays 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Contractors call 303-450-8745 for a portal login. Homeowners create a community-member account. The main line printed on the page is 303-451-8326. Those are city numbers, not tap-to-call for this site.

Utility line not copied from Aurora

The permit page does not say whether you or the city owns the sewer lateral under the parkway. Do not fill that gap with Aurora's tap rule. Ask Northglenn's utility for the address before a sewer repair includes street patching. A one-stop sewer permit is still not a camera, and it is not proof the pipe can be lined.

Questions to settle first

Is a Northglenn water heater a one-stop permit?

The city's list says yes, alongside sewers, furnaces, and air conditioners, through CommunityCore. A residential remodel or addition is on the plan-review clock of 10 to 14 business days. Those are the city's estimates, not a guarantee, and they are not Aurora's same-day request.

Does the city own the lateral?

Northglenn's permit page does not say. Do not assume Aurora's owner-to-the-tap rule or Westminster's driveway rule. Ask the utility that bills the address, and make the quote say who patches the street. Responsibility varies by utility. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

Can a homeowner file?

The page says homeowners create a community-member account, and contractors need a login from 303-450-8745. You can search permits without an account. Filing the wrong account type is how a one-stop heater sits in review. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
