Douglas County

Plumber Roxborough Park CO

Roxborough Park is a census-designated place in Douglas County. Roxborough Water and Sanitation District does not provide the same service in every part of its area, and a Littleton mailing address is not the City of Littleton.

One district, more than one kind of service

Roxborough Water and Sanitation District, at 6222 North Roxborough Park Road, Littleton, phone 303-979-7286, says its central service area, the Roxborough development, receives both water and sewer. Its east area is water only, including Chatfield East, Chatfield Acres, Titan Road Industrial Park, and Plum Valley Heights. Its west area at Lockheed Martin is sewer only. A Roxborough address is not automatically the central area. Ask the district which service, if any, the parcel has before you price a sewer repair that the district may not provide there.

County, city, and ZIP code

Census lists Roxborough Park CDP in Douglas County. If the parcel is unincorporated, the building permit is Douglas County at 100 Third Street, Castle Rock, 303-660-7497. Jefferson County's forms apply only when the parcel is in Jefferson County. The Littleton street address is the district office and a common ZIP code. It is not the City of Littleton's building department, and it is not Pinery Water. Confirm the utility before you authorize work in the street.

Questions to settle first

Does Roxborough Water provide sewer at every Roxborough address?

No. The district says the central area gets water and sewer, the east area is water only, and the Lockheed Martin area is sewer only. Call 303-979-7286 and ask which area the parcel is in. A sewer quote for a water-only account is the wrong job.

Who permits a water heater?

Douglas County, if the parcel is unincorporated, at 303-660-7497. A Littleton ZIP does not make it the City of Littleton. Jefferson County applies only if the parcel is in Jefferson County. The water district is not the building department.

Is Roxborough Park the same as The Pinery?

No. They are different census places and different districts. Pinery Water lists its own communities and office. Roxborough Water describes central, east, and west service that do not match. Douglas County's building counter can be the same only when both parcels are unincorporated Douglas County.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
