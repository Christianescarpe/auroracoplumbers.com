Douglas County

Plumber Sedalia CO

Sedalia is a census-designated place in Douglas County. Sedalia Water and Sanitation District says its primary service is water. That district is not the whole census place, and it is not The Pinery.

A water district with a limited claim

Sedalia Water and Sanitation District describes itself as a Title 32 special district whose primary service is water. The phone on its site is 303-688-2506, and the mailing address is PO Box 222, Sedalia. That is the district's own description. It does not say every house in the Sedalia census place is a customer, and the homepage does not claim sewer service for the whole place. If the bill is this district, call the district about the water line. If the bill is someone else, or the house is on a well, use that record instead.

The building permit is still the county, when the land is unincorporated

Census lists Sedalia CDP in Douglas County. If the parcel is unincorporated, Douglas County Building is at 100 Third Street, Castle Rock, 303-660-7497. That counter is not the Town of Castle Rock, and a Sedalia address is not Pinery Water. Confirm the utility before you authorize work in the street. Responsibility varies by utility.

Questions to settle first

Does Sedalia Water serve every Sedalia address?

No. The district says its primary service is water for its customers. The census place is larger than a sentence on a homepage. Read the bill. A well or another district is still possible. The phone on the district site is 303-688-2506.

Who permits a water heater?

Douglas County, if the parcel is unincorporated, at 303-660-7497. The office address in Castle Rock does not make it a Town of Castle Rock permit. The water district does not issue the building permit. Ask the county to confirm the parcel.

Is the sewer the same district?

Not established by the district's statement that water is its primary service. Do not assume a sewer main. Confirm wastewater the same way you confirm drinking water, from the bill or a septic record, before a camera crew is sent to a city main.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.
