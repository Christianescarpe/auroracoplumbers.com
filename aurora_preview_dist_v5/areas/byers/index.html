Arapahoe County

Plumber Byers CO

The Byers census place is in Arapahoe County, not Adams County. A Byers mailing address still has to be checked parcel by parcel before anyone files a permit.

What the census place is

The Census Bureau's place list records Byers CDP in Arapahoe County. Arapahoe County's Byers Subarea Plan treats the community as unincorporated Arapahoe County and sets its planning area on that census-place boundary. Byers has no town building department. That does not mean every 80103 address is inside the census place. An address outside the place uses the county or city that actually contains the parcel. Do not send a Byers address to Adams County Building Safety because a neighboring community is in Adams County. Watkins is that different case: its census place is in both counties.

Permits, when the parcel is unincorporated Arapahoe

Arapahoe County Building says its services are for unincorporated Arapahoe County and tells applicants to check jurisdiction first. A water heater that is not tankless can be a non-review permit issued when you pay. A tankless heater is a residential remodel: a gas-pipe isometric, the manufacturer's instructions, and a review before issuance. The phone on the county's residential requirements page is 303-795-4400. The office address printed there is 5334 South Prince Street, Littleton. Littleton is where the counter sits. It is not the city the house is in. The same filing split is on the Cherry Creek CDP page.

Water and sewer

Those building instructions do not name a Byers water district, and they do not say that every lot is on a well or a septic system. Confirm the bill, the well, or the septic record before you price a service line or a well pump. Responsibility varies by utility. Aurora's wastewater rules apply to Aurora Water customers, not to a plains address because it is east of the city.

A slow drain is drain cleaning until a camera shows a broken sewer line. A tank swap is water heater replacement. Confirm the permit office and the utility for this address before the work is closed up.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

Who permits a Byers water heater?

Arapahoe County, when the parcel is unincorporated Arapahoe County. The census place is in that county. The building division's number on its residential page is 303-795-4400. A non-tankless heater can be issued when you pay. Tankless is a reviewed remodel. If the parcel is outside the census place, use the county or city that contains it.

Is the sewer a city main?

Not by default. Byers is unincorporated, and the lot may be on a district or a septic system. The building instructions do not pick one. Confirm the bill before you camera a main that may not be there. A septic backup is not an Aurora Water call.

Do I need a permit to change plumbing?

On unincorporated Arapahoe land, plan on a county permit when you replace pipe. A clog that replaces nothing can be different from opening a wall. The non-review path is for a like-for-like heater, not for a new gas line. Call 303-795-4400 if the application type is unclear.
