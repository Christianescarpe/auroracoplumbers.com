Town of Parker · district boundary not settled here

Plumber Parker CO

Inside the Town of Parker, the Building Division permits the work. The water and sewer pipes may be a different agency's, and two of that district's published descriptions do not draw the customer's line in the same place. This page will not pick a winner.

The town line is not the postal city

The Town's building permits page says the Building Division handles permits and inspections within incorporated Parker, and that permits are required for practically any work. Applications go through eTRAKiT. The page's phone is 303-841-0353, at 20120 E Mainstreet. That authority stops at the town limit. A Parker mailing address in unincorporated Douglas County is a county permit, which is the situation described for Highlands Ranch, not an automatic Town of Parker permit.

Do not inherit a sidewalk rule

Parker Water and Sanitation District is not the Town, and it does not serve every address that says Parker. A 2025 district rules document has been described as putting the customer's water, sewer, and fire service line, including the public right of way, on the customer. A district FAQ about lead and service lines has been described as stopping ownership at the property line. Those two readings were not reconciled by calling the district, and they are not reconciled here. No contractor should tell you the sidewalk is always the dividing line, and this guide will not say the customer always owns the pipe under the street.

Ask the district, for the specific tap, which document it will apply. Until you have that answer, a quote for a water line or a sewer line should say "boundary to be confirmed" instead of borrowing Aurora's tap rule or Castle Rock's curb stop. Castle Rock publishes a clearer town-water split. Parker does not, on the documents compared for this page.

An older Town plumbing handout has been indexed with retired code years and with water heaters kept off an over-the-counter list. Those years are not repeated here as the code in force. Use the current Town counter for whether a heater is a simple permit or a review. Aurora's same-day inspection sentence does not apply.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

Who permits a plumbing job in Parker?

The Town of Parker, if the property is inside the incorporated town. The Building Division says it handles permits there and that practically any work needs one. A Parker ZIP outside the town is not that counter. Check the parcel before you assume eTRAKiT at the Town is the right door.

Does the homeowner own the sewer line to the main?

Do not take that as a yes or a no from this page. Parker Water and Sanitation District materials indexed for this project describe the customer line differently, one of them including the public right of way and a FAQ stopping at the property line. Ask the district for the address. Aurora's tap-and-saddle rule is a different city.

Can I use Aurora's water-heater inspection promise?

No, and Aurora does not promise same-day inspection either. Parker has its own building division. An old handout that shuffled water heaters off an over-the-counter path also cited old code years, so it is not quoted here as current. Ask the Town what the heater submittal requires now.
