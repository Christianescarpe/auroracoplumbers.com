Privacy

This guide does not take a form, an upload, or an email. The only referral path intended later is a phone call, and that number is not on the site.

Pages are static. There is no account and no analytics script. The checklist reads boxes in your browser and writes notes on the page. Those notes are not transmitted. Do not type your name, phone, or address into the checklist. If you later call a published number, that call is between you and whoever answers. This website does not record it.

City and town links leave this site and follow those governments' own practices. A future live number would be shown in one place, the same on every page, and this policy would name who receives the call before that happens.

Aurora Plumbing Guide

Aurora Plumbing Guide explains plumbing decisions. It is not a licensed plumbing contractor, and it does not publish a phone number or a request form in this preview. Nothing here confirms that a contractor will accept a Aurora job.
