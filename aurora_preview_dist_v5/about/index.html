About this guide

Aurora Plumbing Guide is a referral-style publication about plumbing decisions in Aurora, Colorado. It is not a plumbing contractor.

The pages name City and Town rules that were read, and they say when a rule was not retrieved or when two documents disagree. They do not invent a crew, a shop, a license number, an insurance certificate, a review score, or years in business. Those facts are not established for this preview, so they are not written.

A contractor you hire is the one who diagnoses, pulls the permit, and does the work. This site does not supervise that contractor. A future referral, if one is approved, would introduce you to an independent company under rules that are not set. Read how it works. The contact page explains why there is nothing to submit.

Content was checked against city pages on November 7, 2026. Codes move. The official page and the inspector outrank this summary.
