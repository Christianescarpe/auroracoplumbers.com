Terms

These terms cover an unpublished draft. They are not a contract for plumbing work and not an offer to refer you to a contractor.

The articles are decision aids. They are not a diagnosis, a permit approval, or a price. Gas, electrical, and excavation hazards are real. Hire someone qualified, or use an owner-builder path only where that building department allows it. This site will not give gas or electrical steps.

City pages can change after November 7, 2026. If a summary and the official page disagree, follow the official page and the inspector. A mailing city is not a jurisdiction. Parker’s service-line documents are called out as unresolved on purpose.
