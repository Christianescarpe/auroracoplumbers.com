How a referral would work

You read the decision, then you call. There is no request form. A phone number is the only way a referral would be placed, and that number is not published until routing is confirmed.

  1. You name the scope

    The repair list separates a clog from a broken sewer, a tank repair from a tankless conversion, and a water leak from a drain. The checklist is notes for you. It does not call anyone and it does not send a message.

  2. A contractor does the work

    Permits, gas, cameras, and excavation belong to the person allowed to perform them where the property sits. This website does not pull the permit and does not stand behind the work.

  3. The call is the referral

    When a number is connected, you would call it. An independent contractor may call you back about that conversation. This preview has no number on the page, no tap-to-call link, and no form to fill out. Geography and hours are not stated because they are not confirmed.

Verify the license of anyone you hire with the Colorado agency that issues plumbing licenses. This page does not link a guessed lookup URL. If the property is in Centennial, Parker, Highlands Ranch, or Castle Rock, the permit office changes with the parcel.

Aurora Plumbing Guide

Aurora Plumbing Guide explains plumbing decisions. It is not a licensed plumbing contractor, and it does not publish a phone number or a request form in this preview. Nothing here confirms that a contractor will accept a Aurora job.
