Water line repair in Aurora

The water service is the pipe that brings drinking water to the building. It is not the sewer lateral, it cannot be filmed the way a drain can, and on an Aurora Water account the repair can include the street.

What Aurora Water's page covers

The City's service-line page says homeowners maintain the service line from the home to the city's main, and the street or sidewalk repair after that work. It says water lines are generally copper and much less likely to break than a failing sewer, and that they cannot be videotaped. "Generally" is doing real work in that sentence. A copper line still splits. A line that is not copper still exists. The page is not a coupon for skipping leak detection.

Do not copy the sewer tap language onto this pipe. The wastewater page's tee and saddle are about the sanitary connection. The water service has its own boundary, and the practical question is whether Aurora Water agrees the leak is on your service or on the main. Ask them, at the address, before you accept a street-cut price.

What the quote has to name

Length and depth. Material, if it can be known without a camera. Where the failure is relative to the meter or the main. Who patches asphalt, curb, and sidewalk. How the house gets water during the repair. What is excluded. A price that says "water line" and does not say whose side of the main is being cut is not comparable to one that does.

Interior pinhole leaks in several rooms are not automatically this yard pipe. Spot repair inside the house is a different scope from the service in the ground. Whole-house replacement of indoor supply piping is broader again, and this guide does not publish that as a separate sales page. Do not let one wet ceiling become a whole-house number without a location.

Other cities draw the line differently. Castle Rock Water tells its customers the Town maintains the water service to the property-line connection and owns the curb stop. Parker does not have a single published boundary this guide will stand behind. Use the city you are actually in.

Restoration and locates

Before anyone digs, the Aurora wastewater page tells owners to call 811 for their sewer service and tap, and the service-line page puts street and sidewalk repair on the owner when the work is the owner's water line. Mark both pipes. A crew that locates only the sewer can still hit the water service, and the other way around. Ask the quote to say the marks were called, who patches, and whether the house will be without water while the section is out. If the leak has not been located, the honest previous step is detection, not a trench length guessed from a wet flower bed.

Questions to settle first

Is the water line the same as the sewer lateral?

No. One is under pressure and brings in drinking water. The other carries wastewater to the city main, and in Aurora you maintain that sewer line through the tap. A camera belongs in the sewer. The water service has to be found another way. Mixing the two quotes is how a street cut gets aimed at the wrong pipe.

Who patches the street in Aurora?

If the cut is your water service, the City's service-line page puts the street or sidewalk repair on the homeowner, along with the pipe from the house to the main. Confirm with Aurora Water that the leak is not on the city main. Ask the quote to include the patch, or to say who else will do it.

Are Aurora water lines always copper?

The City says they are generally copper, and generally less likely to break than a bad sewer. That is not a survey of your yard. Age, soil, and prior repairs still matter. Do not skip location because the page used the word generally, and do not replace a line that has not been shown to fail.

Can I shut the city valve in the parkway?

Not from advice on this site. In Castle Rock the Town says the street valve is theirs and tells customers to use the indoor shutoff, or to call the Town for the curb. Aurora's pages reviewed here do not give a homeowner procedure for the water main valve. Call the utility that serves the address.
