The lot still has a building department

Mobile home plumbing in Aurora

A manufactured home is not a basement house. The pipes are often under the floor, behind skirting, and the fittings may not match a site-built residence. The permit still belongs to the city or county where the lot sits. This guide did not retrieve a separate manufactured-housing plumbing code, and it will not invent one.

Access and parts

Ask whether the repair is above the floor, like a faucet, or under the home. Under-home work needs a safe way in, a sound belly board, and sometimes a different connector than a copper stub in a crawl space. If the skirting has to come off, say who puts it back. A quote that assumes a basement will be wrong on the day.

The water and sewer leaving the home still meet a utility. In Aurora, an Aurora Water customer still has the city's service-line rules for the pipes in the ground: sewer to the tap is the owner's, and the water service to the main is the owner's. The factory-built piping under the floor is not that buried service. Do not blend them into one price without saying which failed. If the lot is in unincorporated Douglas County, the permit counter is the county, as with Highlands Ranch, not a fictional mobile-home city.

What was not verified

HUD or state manufactured-home rules for plumbing alterations were not read for this draft. Do not treat this page as those rules. Ask the building department whether a licensed plumber, a manufacturer-approved method, or both are required before you open the floor. Ordinary fixture repairs that match faucets or toilets may still be the right scope when the failure is the fixture, not the under-home trunk.

Questions to settle first

Does Aurora have a separate mobile-home plumbing permit?

Not one this guide retrieved. The lot is still in a city or a county. An Aurora address uses Aurora's building division. A home on a lot in unincorporated Douglas County uses the county. Ask which parcel you are on before you assume a park office is the permit counter.

Is the pipe under the floor the city's?

No. Under-floor piping belongs with the home. The buried water service and sewer lateral are the utility question. In Aurora those buried lines follow Aurora Water's split, which is not a rule for the belly of the house. The quote should name which pipe failed.

Can you use the same parts as a site-built house?

Not always. Supply connectors under manufactured homes are often a different material and a different fitting. A plumber who brings only house copper may be guessing. Ask what is under there before the parts are purchased. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

Was a special code checked for this page?

No. A manufactured-housing plumbing code was not retrieved. This page only separates access, fittings, and the utility line, and it tells you to ask the building department. It is not a waiver and not a spec. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.
