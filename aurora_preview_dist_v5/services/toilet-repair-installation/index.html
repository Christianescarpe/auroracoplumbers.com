One toilet, not the whole sewer

Toilet repair and installation in Aurora

A toilet that runs, rocks, or leaks at the base is a toilet job. A toilet that fills from below with other drains is a sewer job. Mixing them up buys the wrong repair.

Running, rocking, or leaking at the floor

A constant hiss or a tank that refills by itself is usually the flapper or the fill valve. Water on the floor only at the base, with the bowl otherwise normal, is often the wax ring or the closet bolts, especially if the toilet rocks. Those are repairs or a reset of the same toilet. A cracked bowl or a toilet that is being replaced for height or for a remodel is an installation: a new wax or seal, new bolts, and a check that the flange is sound.

If the flange is broken or sitting below the finished floor, the quote should say so before the new toilet goes down. A pretty toilet on a broken flange leaks into the ceiling of the room below. That is still this page, not a repipe.

When it is not the toilet

Sewage that rises in the bowl when the shower or the laundry runs is not a flapper. In Aurora that can be the building drain or the service line you maintain to the tap. Start with drain cleaning if it is a clog, and sewer repair if a camera shows a break. A sink faucet is a fixture repair. The label that lumps sinks, toilets, and faucets still describes three different parts.

A toilet set on a new floor as part of a bathroom rebuild belongs with the remodel if the flange height changed. Ask who sets the toilet after the floor is done so it is not left out of both quotes.

Questions to settle first

The toilet runs all day. Is the sewer broken?

Probably not. A running toilet refills the tank. The flapper or the fill valve is the usual cause. A broken sewer shows up as a backup, often in more than one fixture or at the lowest drain. Fix the run first. Call it a sewer only if waste is coming the wrong way.

There is water at the base. Do I replace the toilet?

Not automatically. A rock, a loose bolt, or a failed wax ring can wet the floor on a sound bowl. Replace the toilet when the porcelain is cracked or you are changing the fixture. Ask the quote to say whether the flange is included.

Other drains gurgle when this toilet flushes.

That is a vent or a drain problem, not a flapper. A new toilet will not add a vent. If the gurgle comes with a backup, look at the building drain and, in Aurora, the owner-maintained service line. Get a camera before you replace porcelain.

Does a new toilet need a permit?

Swapping a toilet on a sound flange is often handled as a repair. Changing the drain, the flange in a new floor, or the room layout can need a permit from the city or county of the parcel. Englewood lists drain changes as permit work. Ask for your address.
