The unit you already have

Water treatment repair in Aurora

Repair means the filter or softener that is already installed is leaking, stuck, or out of salt and still not working. A new system is a different sale.

Which repair → Quote checklist →

What failed

A softener can stick in regeneration and send water to the drain all day, or it can stop drawing brine. A filter can clog and cut pressure to the house. Ask which of those the technician saw. A clogged filter that drops pressure should not become a repipe. A softener that never should have been installed, because nobody tested the water, is still a repair of that machine, plus a hard look at whether you need it. New equipment is installation.

This guide still has no Aurora hardness number. Do not let a repair visit invent one to sell a larger tank.

Leaks and drains

A unit that overflows can rot the floor. The quote should say whether the leak is a fitting, a valve, or the tank. The drain line should still go to an allowed outlet. If the only symptom is low pressure at one faucet, start with pressure and the faucet, not the softener.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

It is using salt but the water still spots. Is it broken?

Maybe the unit is not regenerating, or it is bypassed, or the spots are not hardness. Ask for a test before and after the unit. Replacing it without that test repeats the original guess. This page will not call the city's water hard.

Water runs to the floor from the softener.

That is a leak or a drain line that came loose, not a reason to repipe the house. Ask which fitting failed. If the unit is in regeneration all day, the control or the valve may be stuck. Those are repairs of this machine.

Should I replace it during the repair?

Only when parts are gone or the tank is failed, and only if you still want treatment after a test. A functioning repair is cheaper to compare when the quote shows the part. A new system is the installation page.

Will a repair fix city pressure?

No. A softener is not the utility's main. If the whole neighborhood is low, call the water utility. If only the pipes after the softener are weak, the unit may be clogged. Say which side of the softener was measured.
