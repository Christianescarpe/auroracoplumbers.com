Several small problems, one visit

General plumbing repair in Aurora

Use this page when you have more than one ordinary fixture problem and none of them is a sewer backup, a gas leak, or a buried water main. It is not a second homepage, and it is not a junk drawer for every pipe in the house.

What fits in one service call

A dripping faucet, a running toilet, a stuck stop, and a sweating supply line under a bath can be one list. The quote should name each item and say what is excluded if something else is found. A trip fee that then becomes a repipe, with no new approval, is not a general repair.

Leave this page when the symptom changes the trade. No hot water is a heater. Sewage at a floor drain is a drain or a service line. A wet yard is leak detection. A smell of gas is gas, and you leave the house first.

Keep the list short enough to price

Write down the room, the fixture, and whether the water is clean or sewage. That is enough for a general-repair visit. It is not enough for a remodel. Moving walls or adding a bathroom is remodel plumbing. An inspection of the whole house with a written report, and no repair that day, is a plumbing inspection.

In Aurora, small fixture repairs often stay repairs. Changing drains, opening walls, or replacing a water heater can need a permit. The heater permit guide is only for the heater. Ask the building department about the rest rather than assuming the faucet list covers a bathroom gut.

Questions to settle first

I have a drip and a running toilet. Is that one job?

Yes, if that is really all. Put both on the list so the quote names each one. It stops being general repair if the plumber finds sewage backing up, a leaking tank shell, or a pipe in the wall. Those have their own pages and their own scope.

Can this visit include the water heater?

Only as a look, unless the quote says repair or replacement. A new tank in Aurora is permitted work, and same-day inspection is not guaranteed. Do not let a 'while we're here' become a heater swap you did not approve.

Is general repair the same as repiping?

No. General repair fixes the fixtures you listed. Repiping replaces supply pipes through the house. One bad faucet is not that project. Ask for the failed part before you approve a whole-house number. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.

What if I smell gas during the visit?

Stop. Leave if the odor is strong, and call the gas utility or emergency services. A general plumbing list does not include opening gas pipe. The gas page explains the boundary. It does not give installation steps. Ask them to write that limit into the quote before you compare it with another company.
