A business, not a spare bath

Commercial plumbing in Aurora

A shop, a kitchen, or an office has occupants, a clock, and usually a longer permit path than a house water heater. The pipe in the ground can still be the owner's.

Time and access

Ask when the water or the drains can be off without stopping the business, and who will be on site to open roofs, ceilings, and locked rooms. A restaurant grease problem is not a house sink. Aurora's service-line page tells people to keep grease out of drains. A commercial quote should say whether a grease interceptor is in scope or excluded. This guide does not invent interceptor sizes.

In Aurora, a building permit for a commercial space goes through plan review. The permitting page points structural permits at that review. It is not the same-day house water-heater path, and even that house path is not guaranteed. Centennial publishes the contrast clearly: a residential water heater can be one-stop, while commercial alterations are listed at 10 business days and new commercial at 15, after a complete submittal. Those are Centennial's targets, not a promise, and they are not Aurora's clock. Use them only for a Centennial address. See Centennial water heater replacement for the house side of that city.

The service line does not become the city's because the customer is a business

Aurora Water's wastewater page covers homes and buildings. The owner still maintains the sewer service line from the tap to the address, including the connection and related street repair. A backup in a first-floor restroom can still be private. Camera it. Lakewood's city sewer crew, at the number on the city's graphic, inspects the city main for its own north Lakewood customers, about 8,000 of them. A Lakewood business on another district is not that crew. Look up the provider.

Key Clarifications

Questions to settle first

Is a commercial water heater a one-stop permit?

Not as a rule you can carry from city to city. Centennial's schedule puts a house water heater on the one-stop list and commercial alterations on a longer review. Aurora's same-day note is about house replacement heaters and is not guaranteed. Ask the building department that has the suite, not the nearest city's chart.

Who owns the sewer under a shop in Aurora?

The building owner still maintains the service line from the tap to the address, including the connection, under Aurora Water's wastewater page. The city keeps the main and the manholes. A backup is not automatically the city's bill. Get a camera and ask where the defect sits.

Can the work wait until after close?

That is a scheduling term, not a code section. Put the hours in the quote: when water can be off, who meets the plumber, and what happens if a part is not on the truck. After-hours work can cost more. This site does not set that rate.

Does Lakewood's 24-hour sewer number cover every business?

No. The city's graphic offers main-line inspection day and night for the city sewer that serves about 8,000 north Lakewood customers. Other districts are on the provider list. Call the utility that actually serves the address, not a number from a different row.
