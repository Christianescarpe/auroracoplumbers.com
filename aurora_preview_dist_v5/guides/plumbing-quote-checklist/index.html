What the briefing is for

It reminds you to name the city, the permit, the pipe, and the exclusions. It does not size a gas line or bless jetting. Read heater permits or sewer ownership when those are the open questions. The repair list is where the hiring pages live.

Questions to settle first

Does this checklist send my information?

No. The buttons build, copy, or print text on this device. There is no form and no address field. If a browser asks to use the clipboard, that copy still stays local. Do not add your name or phone to the printout if you are worried about where the paper goes.

Why is there no price?

A number without the city, the pipe, the permit, and the exclusions is not a quote you can compare. This page lists the questions that change scope. The contractor who sees the property is the one who prices it. Nothing here is a bid from Aurora Plumbing Guide.

What if I do not know whether I am inside Aurora?

Choose "not sure" and make that the first question you ask. An Aurora mailing address can sit outside the city, and Centennial, Parker, Douglas County, and Castle Rock do not share one sewer rule. The briefing will tell you to confirm the permit office before you compare scopes.
